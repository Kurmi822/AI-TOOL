# 🚀 AetherAI – Web3 Project Discovery & Tracking Platform

## 🌟 Overview

**AetherAI** is an AI-powered Web3 discovery platform designed to help users find and track emerging blockchain projects, testnets, airdrops, and community opportunities—all in one place.

Our mission is to make Web3 exploration simpler, faster, and more reliable by combining intelligent data aggregation with trusted verification systems.

---

## 🎯 Why AetherAI?

The Web3 ecosystem is expanding rapidly, making it difficult to:

- ❌ Find legitimate early-stage projects
- ❌ Track active testnets and campaigns
- ❌ Verify project authenticity
- ❌ Discover valuable community opportunities

**AetherAI solves these challenges by providing a single trusted platform for Web3 discovery.**
